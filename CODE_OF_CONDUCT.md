# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

Harassment, sexualized language, personal attacks, trolling, and publication of
another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication
that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary
or permanent ban. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through a
repository-owner contact channel. Maintainers must respect the reporter's privacy
and handle reports fairly.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
